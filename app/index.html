The formulas behind every number

MetricFormulaWhat it tells you
Gross potential rent (GPR)Σ units × monthly rent × 12Rent if every unit is full and paying.
Vacancy & credit lossGPR × vacancy %Rent you won't collect from empty units and bad debt.
Effective gross income (EGI)GPR − vacancy + other incomeWhat actually comes in. Other income is laundry, parking, pet fees, utility bill-backs.
Operating expenses (OpEx)taxes + insurance + owner-paid utilities + repairs + management + payroll/admin + reservesMortgage is not an operating expense.
Net operating income (NOI)EGI − OpExThe property's profit before debt. Value is driven by NOI.
Cap rateNOI ÷ priceUnlevered yield. Higher means cheaper relative to income.
Value at market capNOI ÷ market cap rateEvery $1 of added NOI at a 6% cap adds about $16.67 of value.
Annual debt service12 × loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), r = rate ÷ 12, n = years × 12Principal and interest on the mortgage.
DSCRNOI ÷ annual debt serviceLenders usually want 1.20–1.25 or higher.
Cash needed to closedown payment + closing costs + loan fee + rehab budget + reservesThe capital you have to bring.
Cash-on-cash return(NOI − debt service) ÷ cash neededFirst-year cash yield on your money.
Yield on coststabilized NOI ÷ (price + closing + rehab)Compare against market cap. A spread of 1% or more usually means the value-add pays.
Equity createdstabilized value − all-in costThe value you create by raising NOI.
RUBSowner-paid water/sewer/trash × recapture %Ratio utility billing: charging tenants back a share of utilities.
